BI Property Price-Location Analysis

This business intelligence dashboard analyzes residential property prices in Java by year and province, highlighting market trends, affordability gaps, and practical buying opportunities.

Summary of Residential Price

Over the past five years, median residential prices in Indonesia showed a clear downward trend.

The median price fell from IDR 1.370 billion five years ago to around IDR 830 million in 2024, equal to a decrease of roughly 39.42%.

With lower prices, buyers currently have a stronger chance to secure better-value residential properties before market momentum changes.

Regional Price Comparison in Java

DKI Jakarta remains the most expensive province, supported by the highest mean and upper quartile values in the dataset.

Banten and Jawa Tengah are the most affordable provinces, making them attractive for cost-sensitive buyers.

Across all listed provinces, the overall mean residential price is approximately IDR 943 million.

Key BI Findings

  • Price distribution narrowed over time, with lower quartile and median values in 2024 compared with 2020-2021.
  • Jakarta and Bali continue to sit above the average, while Banten and Jawa Tengah remain below it.
  • Province-level quartile analysis helps distinguish premium markets from affordable residential clusters.
  • The dashboard supports decision-making for buyers, analysts, and property market monitoring use cases.
